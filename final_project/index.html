Our Mission

Financial guidance shouldn't be a luxury reserved for the wealthy.

We started Meridian on a simple premise: too many people make major financial decisions alone, without ever having spoken to someone whose full-time job is to think about money. A free consultation removes the biggest barrier — cost — and lets you find out what good advice actually sounds like before you commit to anything.

Every advisor at Meridian is fee-transparent and fiduciary by standard, meaning we're obligated to act in your interest, not ours. There's no commission tied to what we recommend in your first meeting.
