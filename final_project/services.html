Topics We Cover

Six areas of planning. One conversation to figure out which ones matter to you.

You don't need to know which of these applies to you before you book — that's part of what the first meeting is for. Here's the full range our advisors work in.

01

Budgeting & Cash Flow

A clear picture of what's coming in and going out, and a plan that actually fits how you live — not a rigid spreadsheet you'll abandon in three weeks.

02

Debt Strategy

Prioritizing which balances to pay down first, whether consolidation makes sense, and how to do it without derailing everything else you're saving for.

03

Investment Strategy

Reviewing your current mix, your risk tolerance, and your time horizon — and identifying gaps or overlaps you might not know are there.

04

Retirement Planning

Modeling what retirement could look like at different ages and savings rates, and closing the gap between where you are and where you want to be.

05

Tax-Efficient Planning

Looking at account types, timing, and contribution strategy through a tax lens — not tax filing itself, but the planning that makes filing simpler later.

06

Estate & Legacy Planning

The essentials of making sure your assets go where you intend — beneficiaries, basic estate documents, and how to talk about it with family.

What To Expect

Here's exactly how the meeting goes.

BEFORE
