NP Membership

No Fee

  • Free listing in the online business directory
  • Access to member-only networking events
  • Monthly community newsletter subscription
  • Eligibility to volunteer on Chamber committees

Bronze Membership

$150 / year

  • All NP Membership benefits
  • Standard directory listing with logo
  • One free ticket to the Annual Golf Tournament
  • 10% discount on Chamber-hosted event tickets

Silver Membership

$350 / year

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Eligible for rotating Home Page spotlight placement
  • Two free tickets to the Annual Golf Tournament
  • 20% discount on Chamber-hosted event tickets
  • Access to quarterly member training workshops

Gold Membership

$750 / year

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Priority Home Page spotlight placement
  • Four free tickets to the Annual Golf Tournament
  • 30% discount on Chamber-hosted event tickets
  • Featured business profile in the monthly newsletter
  • One complimentary Business After Hours host slot per year
